# Leisure schedule: prompt and optional interactive tool

**Leisure is a biological and psychological necessity, not a luxury.** It can support mood regulation, attention restoration, and mental balance. This method organizes schedules for restoration, not productivity.

The prompt/template works in any text editor or text-based assistant, on paper, or outside this repository. The browser tool is optional.

## Schedule rules

- Leisure only: no work, study, chores, errands, or obligations disguised as leisure.
- Exactly two session types, using these exact lowercase labels: real-world and digital. Real-world is the default.
- The template and generator organize schedule structure only. They do not invent, prescribe, rank, or prefer activity content; the participant chooses a label or leaves it blank.
- Every digital session needs a short reason and an offline/real-world alternative.
- Every session includes exactly these prompts: “What did it take?”, “What did it give?”, and “Would you repeat it?” (yes / no / with conditions).
- Every schedule includes a weekly review of mood/restoration, cap compliance, and possible offline/real-world alternatives.
- Digital minutes must be no more than 20% of total planned leisure time, or any lower applicable participant/configured/core-protocol limit.
- Show the arithmetic: planned digital minutes ÷ total planned leisure minutes × 100 = digital share. The applicable cap is the lowest of the 20% ceiling, the core protocol's 480-minute weekly maximum, and any lower applicable limit. Revise an over-cap plan before presenting it.
- The cap is a neutral planning constraint, not a judgment that digital leisure is inherently harmful.
- Keep personal constraints and reflections private. Public proposals exclude identifying details and private reflection answers.

## Publication and community review

The required path is **Propose → Review → Approve → Publish**. The interactive tool prepares a draft only; an authorized human maintainer must explicitly approve the exact proposed version before merge and publication in 01-KITS/community-schedules/.
