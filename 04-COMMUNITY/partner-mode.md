# Partner mode (optional)

Off by default. You switch it on, and you can switch it off.

**What it is.** One person you choose sees your weekly page and asks one question about the week.

**What the partner does.** Listens. Asks a single open question. Does not set limits, check numbers, or step in.

**What the partner does not do.** Judge a score. Pause an activity for you. Bring it up at other times unless you do.

**Good questions.** What surprised you this week? Which session would you repeat? What got in the way of the one you skipped?

**Agree these first.** How often you share, and what happens if you want to stop sharing. Stopping needs no reason.
