# The seven-day start

A shared way to begin, alone or with others. It doubles as the community challenge: everyone starts on the same day and compares notes, not scores.

| Day | Do |
|---|---|
| 1 | List three activities you already do. Use a timer only. |
| 2–3 | After each session answer three questions: did it restore me, did it touch my work, was it hard to stop? |
| 4–5 | Add one window for leisure and the daily cap. |
| 6 | Fill one full session card. |
| 7 | Do your first weekly page. |

## Sharing

If you share, share a card or a sentence. Do not share another person's numbers. A shared score is a snapshot of a single week, not a ranking.

Suggested tag: `#ProtocolX`

## What not to do

Do not compare scores to win. A 5 for someone with a hard week can be better than a 9 for someone coasting. Compare what you noticed.
