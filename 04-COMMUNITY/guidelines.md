# Community guidelines

1. **Share what you noticed, not what you scored.** Numbers are yours.
2. **No medical claims.** Protocol X does not diagnose or treat anything. Do not give or ask for medical advice here. If someone describes real distress, point them to a qualified professional.
3. **No ranking people.** Do not compare, shame or reward scores.
4. **Respect other people's data.** Do not post someone else's cards or notes without their say.
5. **Translations welcome.** Keep formulas, thresholds and the safety gate unchanged. See `06-LOCALIZATION`.
6. **Disagree about the rules openly.** Open an issue with what you changed and what happened.
