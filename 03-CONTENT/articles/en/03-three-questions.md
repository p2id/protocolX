# Three questions after any session

You do not need the full card on day one. You need three questions: Did it restore me? Did it touch my work? Was it hard to stop?

They map to three readings: Vitality, Spill and Pull. Answer each with a number from 0 to 10, or just high, middling or low. A rough answer you give is worth more than a precise one you skip.

Notice which question you want to avoid. People tend to have one. It is often the most useful of the three.

## One step

Write the three questions on a sticky note and put it where your leisure usually starts.

## One question

Which of the three do I least want to answer?
