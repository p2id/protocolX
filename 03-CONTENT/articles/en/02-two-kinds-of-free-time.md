# Two kinds of free time

There are hours you leave fuller and hours you leave emptier. During the hour they can feel identical. The difference arrives later, when you try to start something else.

Protocol X calls the first reading Vitality. You rate it from 0 to 10, but not straight away. You wait four to twenty-four hours and ask one plain question: am I more able to do the next thing than before?

Do this for a handful of sessions and a pattern appears without any effort. Some activities sit at 7 and 8 every time. Some sit at 3. You do not have to decide anything yet. You only have to see it.

## One step

Rate Vitality for three sessions this week, four to twenty-four hours after each one.

## One question

Which of these would I choose again if I knew how I would feel afterwards?
