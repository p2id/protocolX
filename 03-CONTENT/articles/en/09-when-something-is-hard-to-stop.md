# When something is hard to stop

Pull is the fourth reading. It asks one thing: how hard was it to stop? Zero means you were done when you were done. Ten means it kept returning to your mind afterwards.

A high Pull is not a verdict on you. It is information about an activity, in a certain place in your day, at a certain length. Often the fix is not to remove it. It is to give it a smaller window, or a different hour.

This is also why the timer alerts at 80%. It leaves you a last stretch to choose the stop yourself, before the end chooses for you.

## One step

Once this week, stop at the 80% alert and notice the first thing you feel.

## One question

What was I doing at the moment the timer sounded?
