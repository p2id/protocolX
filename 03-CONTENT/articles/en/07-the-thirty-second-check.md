# The thirty-second check

Some days the full card is too much. For those days Protocol X has a fast mode: three questions, thirty seconds, no scores.

Ask them out loud if it helps. Restored, neutral or drained. Touched work, or not. Easy or hard to stop. Write three words and move on.

Fast mode will not give you a weekly average. It gives you something better for a busy week: the habit of looking at all. You can add numbers on a calmer day.

## One step

Use fast mode after your next two sessions and keep the three words each time.

## One question

Would I have noticed this without writing it down?
