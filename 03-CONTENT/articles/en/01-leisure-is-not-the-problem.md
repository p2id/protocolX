# Leisure is not the problem

Most advice about free time sorts activities into good and bad. Reading is good. Scrolling is bad. Then you read for an hour on Tuesday and come out lit up, and read for an hour on Friday and come out flat.

The category tells you what you did. It does not tell you what it did to you. Protocol X moves the question from "which activity" to "what did it leave behind". That is a smaller question, and you can answer it in ten seconds.

Nothing here asks you to feel guilty about rest. Rest can be generous. The only aim is to notice, session by session, which hours give something back and which quietly take it.

## One step

Take yesterday's longest leisure session and write one word for how you felt an hour after it ended.

## One question

What did that hour leave in me?

*Protocol X is a self-observation practice. It is not medical advice.*
