# The first hour of work

Protocol X has one rule about work: keep the first and last hour free of leisure. It is a small rule and it does a surprising amount.

The first hour sets the direction of the day. Fill it with something that asks nothing of you and the rest of the day starts from there. The last hour does the opposite job. It lets you finish instead of drifting out.

This is not a rule about productivity. It protects a window you chose. If the window is yours, you will notice quickly how often you reach for something to fill it.

## One step

Tomorrow, name the time before which you will not open any leisure, and keep to it once.

## One question

What do I reach for first when I sit down to work?
