# 20/20 is a technique, not a rule

The 20/20 technique is a trade. Twenty minutes of digital leisure, then twenty minutes of focused work before you allow more. Leisure has to earn the next leisure.

It suits the days when you feel pulled toward the screen and cannot say why. It does not suit a film, a match, or an evening with friends. Those have their own shape, and cutting them into twenty-minute pieces would spoil what you came for.

That is why it is optional. Use it as a tool for a specific kind of day, not as a rule for every day.

## One step

Try it once, on a day you notice yourself reaching for the screen without choosing to.
