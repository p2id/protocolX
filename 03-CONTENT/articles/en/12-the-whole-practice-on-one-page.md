# The whole practice on one page

Set apart. Choose an activity, a window and a length, and start a timer that alerts at 80%. Use. Stop at the timer. Read. Rate Pull and Spill straight away, then Vitality, Clarity and Give four to twenty-four hours later. Review. Once a week, average the sessions by minutes.

The score is a weighted mean of the five readings, with Pull and Spill counted in reverse. If Pull reaches 7 or Spill reaches 6, the score is capped at 3.9. Give can be N/A when it does not apply.

Change at most one rule a month. Write down what you changed. Everything else on this site is an optional extra.

## One step

Print the one-page card from the kits folder and put it where you will see it.
