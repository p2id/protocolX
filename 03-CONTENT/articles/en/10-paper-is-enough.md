# Paper is enough

You can run Protocol X on an index card. Draw five boxes and label them Vitality, Clarity, Give, Pull and Spill. Add a line for the activity and one for the minutes.

Paper has real advantages here. It has no notifications. It does not ask for an account. It will not change its design in a year. And the small friction of writing a number by hand makes you mean it.

Use an app, a spreadsheet or the tools on this site if you prefer them. The practice does not depend on any of it.

## One step

Draw the five boxes on a card and keep it where you keep your remote or your phone charger.

## One question

What is the least effort at which I would actually keep doing this?
