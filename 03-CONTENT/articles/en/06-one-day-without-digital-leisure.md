# One day without digital leisure

Protocol X suggests one day a week without digital leisure. Treat it as a baseline, not a punishment, and not a cleanse.

On that day you find out what you reach for at four in the afternoon. You find out what you do with a gap when the usual option is missing. The answer is rarely dramatic, and it is nearly always informative.

Choose the replacement in advance. A walk, a book on paper, a phone call, cooking something slowly. A day with nothing planned tends to be refilled by habit.

## One step

Pick the day and write down the one thing you will do in place of your usual first reach.

## One question

What did I reach for at four o'clock?
