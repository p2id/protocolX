# A partner, not a guard

Protocol X has an optional partner. It is off by default, and you switch it on yourself. A partner is one person you choose who sees your weekly page.

Their job is small. They ask a single question about the week, and then they listen. They do not set limits, check your numbers, or step in. A partner supports. They do not control.

If you would rather not share, that is a complete way to use the practice. If you do share, choose the person for how they listen, not for how strict they are.

## One step

Decide yes or no on a partner. If yes, name the one question you would want them to ask.

## One question

Who in my life asks questions without already having an answer?
