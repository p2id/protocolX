# When rest stops working

Sometimes the numbers say it before you do. Vitality is 3. Clarity is 3. Next session, the same. The activity is still pleasant, and still not working.

In Protocol X that pattern has a plain label: draining. The suggestion is modest. Do not drop it all at once. Replace a quarter of it this week with something that restored you before, and look again in seven days.

This is a pattern in your own notes, not a diagnosis of anything. If you feel unwell in ways that go beyond tiredness, a practice like this is the wrong tool. Speak with someone qualified.

## One step

Look at your last five sessions. Find any activity with Vitality and Clarity both at 4 or below.

## One question

What did I use to do that gave me something back?
