Leisure Loop

Free dynamic daily and weekly leisure ideas. Plan for enjoyment, not productivity. Real-world activities are the default; digital leisure is optional.

1. Personalize your week

Effective cap is the lower of your maximum and 20% of planned leisure.

Avoid names, addresses, or private details.

2. Plan totals

Planned leisure315 min
20% digital ceiling63 min
Effective digital cap60 min
Planned digital15 min

Each digital session needs a reason.

3. Edit the daily schedule

Edit activity, type, duration, reason, offline alternative, and session review. Digital totals update as you edit.

4. Weekly review (kept private)

5. Share a public schedule

Copy or download a Markdown version, or prepare a GitHub contribution. Personal constraints and weekly reflection answers are excluded from the public copy. GitHub asks you to review and submit the proposal; nothing is published without your action.

Repository: p2id/protocolX. Approved schedules should be reviewed before inclusion in the shared collection. No GitHub token is stored in this browser.

Reuse and adapt. Keep activities low-cost, accessible, safe and non-competitive. A missed session is information, not failure. Digital leisure is not inherently bad.
