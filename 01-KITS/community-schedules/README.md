# Community schedule proposals

This folder is the **canonical collection of maintainer-approved public schedules**. Do not put unreviewed submissions here.

## Required publication path

**Propose → Review → Approve → Publish**

1. **Propose:** submit an anonymized, editable Markdown schedule through a pull request or the documented proposal process. A generator's contribution button creates a draft only; a person must review and submit it.
2. **Review:** check leisure-only scope and the necessity framing; exactly two session types with exact lowercase labels real-world and digital; real-world default; participant choice without prescribed or ranked activity content; self-sufficiency; accessibility and safety; a short reason and offline/real-world alternative for every digital session; visible digital-cap arithmetic; the three exact post-session prompts; weekly mood/restoration review; privacy; portability; standalone usability; and consistency with applicable core rules.
3. **Approve:** an authorized human maintainer must explicitly approve the exact proposed version. Community feedback, successful validation, an open pull request, or an automation run is not approval.
4. **Publish:** only after verifying approval for the exact version may it be merged into this folder. No tool or workflow may auto-publish generated schedules here.

## Proposal format

Use the [portable schedule prompt and template](../daily-and-weekly-leisure-schedule.md). Give a file a descriptive, non-branded name such as quiet-low-energy-week.md. Include audience or adaptation notes only when useful and non-identifying.

Every schedule must show:
- Total planned leisure minutes, planned digital minutes, and the formula: planned digital minutes ÷ total planned leisure minutes × 100 = digital share.
- The 20% ceiling and the applicable cap, including any lower participant/configured/core-protocol limit.
- Confirmation that the share is at most 20% and digital minutes are within the applicable cap.
- Exactly one type per session, labelled real-world or digital.
- A short reason and an offline/real-world alternative for every digital session.
- Exactly these three post-session prompts for every session: “What did it take?”, “What did it give?”, and “Would you repeat it?” (yes / no / with conditions).
- A weekly review of mood/restoration, cap compliance, and possible offline/real-world alternatives.

Before submitting, remove names, precise locations, contact details, sensitive information, and private post-session reflection answers. Keep the method self-sufficient, portable, editable, and usable without the repository. Do not imply that one schedule fits everyone.

## Canonical collection

Only maintainer-reviewed and approved schedules belong here. The folder may remain empty until a suitable proposal passes review.
