What it is

Protocol X treats leisure as something you set apart from the rest of your day, use deliberately, and keep within limits you chose. It does not rank activities by type. A podcast, a match, a walk or a feed can each restore you one day and drain you the next.

So it asks about the aftermath. Did you come back with more energy? Clearer? Able to give something? Was it hard to stop? Did it spill into your work? Five answers, one score, one weekly look.

One session

  1. Set apart. Pick one activity, a window and a duration. Start a timer that alerts at 80%.
  2. Use. Do it inside the window. Stop at the timer.
  3. Read. Straight after, rate Pull and Spill. Four to twenty-four hours later, rate Vitality, Clarity and Give.
  4. Review. Once a week, average the sessions by minutes. Change at most one rule a month.

Safety gate. If Pull is 7 or more, or Spill is 6 or more, the score is capped at 3.9 and the session is labelled Harmful, however well it did elsewhere. Bands: 8.0–10 Enhancing, 6.0–7.9 Acceptable, 4.0–5.9 Warning, below 4.0 Harmful.

Questions

Do I need an app?

No. A timer and a printed session card are enough. A spreadsheet, a notes app or a chat window also works.

Is Protocol X a medical or diagnostic tool?

No. It is a self-observation practice. Pull is your own rating of how hard a session was to stop, not a clinical measure. If a habit is harming your health, work or relationships, speak with a qualified professional.

How is the score calculated?

Score = 0.30×VRI + 0.25×CEI + 0.15×CQI + 0.15×(10−ARI) + 0.15×(10−WII). If Pull (ARI) is 7 or more, or Spill (WII) is 6 or more, the score is capped at 3.9 and the session is labelled Harmful.

What if a reading does not apply, like Give after a solo walk?

Mark Give as N/A. It drops out of the calculation and the other four weights scale up in proportion.

Does it send my data anywhere?

No. The tools run in your browser and save to your own device only. You can export or delete everything at any time.

What does it cost, and can I adapt it?

It is free and released under CC0. You may copy, change, translate and sell adaptations without asking. Credit is welcome, never required.

How do I start?

Spend seven days on one rule at a time: list three activities, answer three quick questions after each session, then add one window, a daily cap and your first full card.
